Discover the advanced AI technology behind our baby cry classification system. Learn about the hybrid neural architecture, training methodology, and performance metrics that power intelligent cry analysis.

Project Overview

Our system intelligently classifies five distinct baby cry categories to provide actionable insights for caregivers:

  • Belly Pain — Indicates gas, colic, or digestive discomfort
  • Cold/Hot — Temperature-related discomfort signals
  • Discomfort — General issues like diaper, clothing, or positioning
  • Hungry — Clear feeding and nutrition signals
  • Tired — Overstimulation or sleep requirement indicators

Hybrid Neural Architecture

Our model employs multiple specialized branches to capture diverse acoustic features:

  • Mel-spectrogram CNN — Extracts spectral structure and frequency patterns
  • BiLSTM with Attention — Models temporal dynamics and sequence patterns
  • Traditional Features — MFCC, spectral, and rhythm descriptors
  • Wav2Vec2 Embeddings — High-level semantic audio representations

These branches are fused through a dense layer for robust multi-modal classification.

Dataset & Training Strategy

  • Data Sources — Curated cry datasets from Kaggle and public research corpora
  • Balancing — Stratified sampling and SMOTE for class equilibrium
  • Augmentation — Pitch/time shifts, background noise, tempo variations
  • Preprocessing — Standardized to 16 kHz, 1–10 second audio segments
  • Validation — K-fold cross-validation with temporal splits

Important Considerations
