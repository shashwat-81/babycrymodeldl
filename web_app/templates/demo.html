Tips for Best Results

Audio Quality
  • Clear audio without background noise
  • Duration: 3-10 seconds recommended
  • Sample rate: 16kHz or higher
Recording Conditions
  • Record close to the baby
  • Minimize environmental noise
  • Capture the full cry pattern

Feature Analysis

The model analyzes multiple aspects of baby cries:

MFCC Features

Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients capture the spectral envelope and are crucial for speech-like analysis.

Spectrograms

Visual representation of frequency content over time, processed by CNN layers.

Spectral Features

Spectral centroid, rolloff, and bandwidth describe the frequency distribution.

Rhythm Features

Tempo and onset patterns capture the rhythmic structure of cries.
